About RaC CIT

Carrying Forward a Legacy of Service

The Rotaract Club of Coimbatore Institute of Technology is one of the oldest and most active student-led service organizations within CIT.

Chartered on 14 January 1977, the club has consistently worked towards empowering students through leadership, fellowship and impactful community service initiatives.

Supported by the Rotary Club of Coimbatore East, RaC CIT nurtures young leaders who strive to create sustainable social impact through projects, professional development activities, and meaningful collaborations.

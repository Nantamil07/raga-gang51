Rotaract Club of Coimbatore Institute of Technology

The Rotaract Club of Coimbatore Institute of Technology is a community of young leaders dedicated to service, fellowship, and self-development. Guided by the principles of Rotary, our members come together to create meaningful impact within the campus and the wider community, building lifelong friendships along the way.

Rotaract Prayer

O God, our Father, whose beloved son taught us to know that whosoever would be greatest among us should be servant of all — teach us the ways of gentleness and peace, that all people may acknowledge the kingdom of good will and understanding. May we, as Rotaractors, strive to be the light of service in every heart we touch.

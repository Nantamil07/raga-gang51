About the Club

The Rotaract Club of Coimbatore Institute of Technology has been a beacon of leadership, fellowship and service since its charter in 1977. The club empowers students to create meaningful impact through community service, professional development and collaborative initiatives.

Guided by Rotary values, RaC CIT continues to inspire generations of changemakers who believe in “Service Above Self” while strengthening friendships through fellowship.

Rotaract Prayer

O Lord and Giver of all good, We thank You for our Rotaract Club. Bless us with wisdom, humility, courage and compassion. Inspire us to serve humanity with dedication and integrity. Strengthen our fellowship and guide us to become responsible leaders.
